# Ballpark Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Ballpark is a meal and macro tracking app. The app is designed to store your meal history, nutrition goals, settings, and synced weight data locally on your device. Ballpark does not include advertising, does not sell personal data, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Data Stored On Your Device

Ballpark stores the following data locally on your iPhone:

- meal entries
- estimated calories and macro nutrients
- meal titles, summaries, and assumptions
- nutrition goals
- app settings
- Apple Health weight entries, if you choose to enable Apple Health weight sync

This data is used to provide the app's core features, including daily summaries, history views, weight trends, maintenance calorie estimates, backup export, and CSV export.

## Meal Text, Photos, And Audio

Ballpark can estimate meals from text descriptions, selected photos, and optional voice input.

If you choose to use AI processing, Ballpark sends the relevant meal input through Ballpark's backend service to OpenAI so the app can generate meal estimates or improve voice transcription.

This may include:

- meal descriptions you type
- selected meal photos
- optional recorded audio when OpenAI transcription is enabled
- app language and unit preferences
- a random installation identifier used for rate limiting and service reliability

Meal photos are used for the current estimate and are not saved by the app after the estimate is complete.

You can choose not to use AI processing. Manual meal entry remains available without OpenAI processing.

## OpenAI Processing

When AI processing is enabled, meal inputs pass through Ballpark's backend service to OpenAI only for the purpose of estimating meals or improving transcription.

Ballpark's backend service is designed not to persist the contents of meal text, photos, or audio. OpenAI may process and retain data according to OpenAI's applicable API data policies, including for abuse monitoring or legal compliance where required.

You can learn more about OpenAI's privacy practices here:

https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

## Apple Health

If you enable Apple Health weight sync, Ballpark requests read-only access to body weight data from Apple Health.

Ballpark uses Apple Health weight data only to show weight trends and estimate maintenance calories. Ballpark does not write data to Apple Health, does not send Apple Health weight data to OpenAI, and does not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or tracking.

You can manage or revoke Health permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

## Camera, Photos, Microphone, And Speech Recognition

Ballpark may request access to:

- the camera, so you can take meal photos
- selected photos, so you can choose meal images for estimation
- the microphone, so you can record voice input
- speech recognition, so voice input can be converted to text

These permissions are used only for the features you choose to use.

## Backups And Exports

Ballpark lets you export your data.

JSON backups may include meal entries, goals, settings, day statuses, and synced weight entries. CSV exports contain meal data for analysis.

Exports are created only when you request them. After export, you control where those files are saved or shared.

## Deletion And Retention

You can delete meal entries in the app. You can also clear saved meal data from Settings.

Data stored locally remains on your device unless you delete it, uninstall the app, or restore/change device backups through Apple system features.

Ballpark's backend service is designed not to store meal text, photos, or audio contents after processing. Basic technical logs may be used to operate, secure, debug, and rate-limit the service.

## No Advertising Or Tracking

Ballpark does not show third-party ads.

Ballpark does not use data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Ballpark does not sell your personal data.

## Approximate Nutrition Information

Ballpark's nutrition estimates are approximate. They are not medical advice and should not be used to diagnose, treat, or manage a medical condition.
