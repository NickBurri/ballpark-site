# Ballpark Support

Ballpark is an iPhone app for simple meal, calorie, and macro tracking.

If you need help, want to report a problem, or have a suggestion, contact:

[ballparkmacrotracker@icloud.com](mailto:ballparkmacrotracker@icloud.com)

Please include:

- a brief description of the problem
- your iPhone model and iOS version
- the version of Ballpark you are using
- a screenshot of the error, if appropriate

Please do not send meal photographs, health information, purchase receipts, or other sensitive personal information by email.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Ballpark AI does not recognize my subscription

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Open Ballpark, go to **Settings**, open **Ballpark AI**, and select **Restore Subscription**.

If the problem continues, restart the app and try again later. You can also contact support using the email address above.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can view or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings:

[Manage Apple subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions)

### How do I restore a subscription?

Open Ballpark, go to **Settings**, open **Ballpark AI**, and select **Restore Subscription**.

### Can I use Ballpark without a subscription?

Yes. Manual meal and macro tracking remains available without a Ballpark AI subscription.

### How do I delete my data?

Ballpark stores meal history, goals, settings, and imported Apple Health weight data locally on your device.

In Ballpark, open **Settings → Data Management** to delete meal data or locally cached weight data. Uninstalling Ballpark also removes its locally stored data, subject to Apple device-backup behavior.

### How do I withdraw consent for AI processing?

Open **Settings → Privacy & Data** in Ballpark and disable OpenAI processing. Manual meal tracking remains available.
